## Style Prompt

Create a warm, professional finance explainer for "九点猫研 CatDesk 9" in the existing yesp cat product style. The visual language should feel like a tidy analyst desk with playful cat stickers: cream paper, blush panels, mint confidence states, crisp dark-brown text, stamped paw details, and real dashboard screenshots as the proof layer. The tone is calm, reliable, and lightly cute, not meme-heavy.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#fff7ef`
- Paper: `#fffdf8`
- Ink: `#2d201b`
- Muted text: `#846f67`
- Blush accent: `#ff7890`
- Mint positive: `#12805d`
- Lavender signal: `#7e68f2`
- Border: `#ead8cb`

## Typography

- Headlines: `serif`
- Body: `sans-serif`
- Data labels: `monospace`

## Motion Rules

- Use soft blur crossfades and one push-like screenshot move, matching a measured product demo.
- Entrances are staggered with varied easing: `power3.out`, `back.out(1.3)`, `expo.out`, `sine.out`.
- Keep screenshots readable; animate their wrappers rather than changing the image dimensions.
- Keep captions in one lower band, never covering the core dashboard detail.

## What NOT to Do

- No dark cyber-finance palette.
- No blue-purple gradient hero treatment.
- No exaggerated meme motion or bouncing every element.
- No fake stock-buy signal language; keep it framed as research support.
- No tiny subtitle text below 30px in the final 1920x1080 render.
